A small daily loop

Plan ahead. Act in the present.

01

Set your rhythm

Add routines for the days or weeks that fit you, and one-time tasks when something new comes up.

02

Choose today

Slide right for Done or left to skip. Skip today closes today’s card; Skip this week closes the weekly occurrence; Skip task resolves a one-time task.

03

Notice patterns

Review recent days and see which tasks you completed, skipped, or left unfinished.

Private by default

Your plans stay with you.

Sliding Tasks works offline, with optional file exports and automatic backups. Tasks and history stay on your device unless you export them or choose a backup folder. There is no account, ads, product analytics, or app sync service.
