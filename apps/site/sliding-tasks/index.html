A small daily loop

Plan ahead. Act in the present.

Every choice has a place

Skip is a decision.

Done means you did it. Skip means you chose not to. What happens next depends on your plan.

Routine

“Not today.”

Take a short walk on your scheduled days. Done or Skip today closes today’s card. A fresh card appears on the next scheduled day.

Once per week

“Not this week.”

Review priorities on any of your selected days. Done or Skip this week closes the whole active week. The task returns in the next active week on your schedule.

One-time

“Let this task go.”

Book a dentist appointment when you’re ready. Done finishes the task; Skip task closes it because you chose not to do it. Either way, it stops returning.

Left it unanswered?

A card left open when its day ends is recorded as Missed. A routine returns on its next scheduled day; an unresolved one-time task carries forward. A once-per-week task can return on the next eligible day until you choose Done or Skip that week.

For example: make Tuesday and Friday available for reviewing priorities. Leave Tuesday unanswered and it appears Friday. Choose Done or Skip on Tuesday and it waits until the next active week. Weeks run Monday to Sunday; you choose how often they repeat.

Try it in the app: the optional practice guide takes you through Plan, Today, Undo, and Review with examples that stay separate from your real tasks.

Private by default

Your plans stay with you.

Sliding Tasks works offline, with optional file exports and automatic backups. Tasks and history stay on your device unless you export them or choose a backup folder. There is no account, ads, product analytics, or app sync service.
