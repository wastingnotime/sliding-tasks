# Sliding Tasks privacy policy

Effective September 23, 2026.

Sliding Tasks is provided by Wasting No Time. It works offline and does not
require an account.

## Information stored on your device

Your task titles, schedules, card decisions, and history are stored on your
device. The current app does not send them to Wasting No Time or other
companies. It has no ads, analytics, or sync service.

## How to erase your information

You can remove individual planned tasks in the app. To erase all Sliding Tasks
data, use Android Settings → Apps → Sliding Tasks → Storage → Clear storage, or
uninstall the app. Device backup is disabled for this app.

## Changes to this policy

If the app later adds sync, analytics, or other data processing, this policy
will be updated before those features are released.
