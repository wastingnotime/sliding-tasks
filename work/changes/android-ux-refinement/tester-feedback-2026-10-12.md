# Tester feedback and launch decision — October 12, 2026

Tracking: [WNT-117](https://linear.app/wastingnotime/issue/WNT-117).

The owner reported that testers considered Sliding Tasks easy to use, and that
the video walkthroughs and practical guide helped substantially. Once per week
was less intuitive at first because this planning model is unusual in task apps.

The owner accepts the distinction as an intentional product choice. Routine and
One-time address familiar needs. Once per week becomes useful when someone has
a task that can be handled on any eligible day but only needs one resolution
within an active week. Keep all three choices available for the initial public
release. Consider making Once per week an advanced feature in the future if
later evidence indicates that few people use it. This is a possible future
direction, not a current implementation request or a measured usage finding.

This is qualitative feedback reported by the owner. Tester counts, timings,
individual protocol outcomes and accessibility coverage were not supplied.
The owner accepted the feedback as sufficient for launch and authorized
publishing the tested 0.3.5 bundle to production. WNT-117 was marked Done.
